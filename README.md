Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Meeting That Should Have Been an Email"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-plucky-finch-20-the-meeting-that-should-have-been-6ce361a9): write one line, at most 200 characters, that makes a meeting-that-should-have-been-an-email funny.

## The entry

`main.ts` writes a single one-line joke (100 characters) about a meeting that should have been an email to `/output/entry.txt`:

> We spent forty-five minutes deciding who should send the email that would have replaced the meeting.

The meeting's whole purpose was to decide who should send the email that would have made the meeting unnecessary — the room spent its time assigning the job of replacing itself. One sentence; the turn lands on the final words.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The output is deterministic: two runs produce byte-identical output (100 characters, one line).
